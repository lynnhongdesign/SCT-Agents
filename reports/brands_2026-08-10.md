# Agent 3: Brands & Companies — Weekly Dog Industry Intelligence (week of Aug 10, 2026)

Coverage below was sourced via targeted web searches across Pet Food Processing, Pet Age, PR Newswire, GlobeNewswire, and financial/earnings coverage instead, with publish dates verified per item.

- **[Brand move] Freshpet posts strong Q2 2026, raises full-year guidance** — *Freshpet, Inc. investor release / Pet Food Processing coverage, published Aug 5, 2026.* Net sales rose **15.5% year-over-year to $305.6M**, driven by volume growth of 15.7% (price/mix was slightly negative). Adjusted gross margin hit **48.6%**, its highest level since early 2020. The company raised its FY2026 outlook to **10–12% sales growth** and **$210–220M adjusted EBITDA**, and shares jumped roughly 8% in premarket trading on the news.

- **[Brand move] The Farmer's Dog acquires Woof, expanding beyond nutrition into enrichment/wellness** — *PR Newswire / Pet Age / PetfoodIndustry, published Aug 6, 2026.* The Farmer's Dog signed a definitive agreement to acquire Woof, a 2019-founded pet wellness brand best known for the "Pupsicle," a refillable enrichment/wellness device. The move is explicitly framed as extending the fresh-food company's reach "beyond the bowl" into functional enrichment products. Financial terms were not disclosed; close expected later in 2026.

- **[Brand move] BARK Inc. reports steep revenue decline but improved profitability in Q1 FY2027** — *Earnings coverage (MarketBeat/Seeking Alpha/Yahoo Finance), published Aug 7, 2026.* Quarterly revenue fell to **$78.8M from $102.9M a year earlier (down ~23%)**, driven by a shrinking DTC subscriber base. Despite the decline, adjusted EBITDA turned to a positive **$600K (vs. $100K prior year)**, subscriber retention improved over 170 basis points, and average order value rose $0.45/unit. Management guided Q2 revenue of $83–85M and said it expects DTC to return to growth in the back half of fiscal 2027, aided in part by new product launches.

- **[Brand move] Three Dog Brands (Three Dog Bakery) to unveil 7 new treats at SuperZoo 2026** — *Pet Food Processing, published ~Aug 7, 2026.* Ahead of SuperZoo (Aug 12–14, Las Vegas), the company detailed specific new SKUs: two additions to its Bark'n Crunch! line — Turkey Chips and Chicken & Hickory Smoke Chips (100% turkey, positioned for sensitive stomachs/picky eaters) — plus a new Chicken Pot Pie treat in its biscuits/cookies portfolio, hitting shelves in September. Positioned around three trend buckets: protein-forward, "people-inspired" flavors, and small-dog-focused treats.

- **[Brand move] Independent adventure-gear brand Wilderdog lands national REI retail partnership** — *Outdoor Sports Wire, published Aug 3, 2026.* Wilderdog, a small/independent-style dog gear brand (harnesses, leashes, adventure accessories), announced a new wholesale partnership placing its products in **40 REI Co-op stores nationwide**, with plans to expand further. Notable as a smaller, DTC-rooted brand successfully breaking into a major specialty-outdoor retail channel — a relevant channel-expansion model for smaller sellers eyeing offline growth.
