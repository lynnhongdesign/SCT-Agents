## 1. Apparel & Wear

- **Market size/growth:** The North America pet clothing market is projected to grow from roughly $2.03 billion (2025) to $2.12 billion in 2026, with the global pet clothing market on pace to hit ~$6.6 billion by end-2026 and $11.1 billion by 2036 (5.3% CAGR 2026–2036); coats/jackets are expected to be the largest product segment (34% share) and dogs the largest pet-type segment (61% share) in 2026. — Future Market Insights, "Pet Clothing Market" report (2026 edition).
- A narrower "dog apparel" definition puts the global category at roughly $1.22–$1.27 billion in 2026, up ~10.1% year-on-year. — Global Growth Insights, "Dog Apparels Market Size & Trends" (2026 report).
- **Funding/M&A:** No fresh (past 2 weeks) dog-apparel-specific funding or M&A activity turned up after broadening searches — this category appears quiet on the deal front this week; the news flow is mostly product/collection launches rather than capital events.
- **Brand to watch:** Little Beast — an NYC-founded dog apparel label known for bold prints/colors (a deliberate break from the category's muted norms) and a run of streetwear-adjacent collaborations, most recently with Madhappy (announced December 15, 2025). Its TikTok-driven following continues to make it the go-to collaborator for "cool" fashion brands entering pet apparel. — WWD / brand collaboration coverage.

## 2. Supplements & Wellness

- **Market size/growth:** The global pet dietary supplements market is estimated at $5.11 billion in 2026, growing at a 7.42% CAGR, with dogs capturing roughly 77% of dietary-supplement share (2025 data). A separate, broader "pet supplements" market estimate puts 2026 global value at $3.33 billion, with dogs at ~49.6% share. — Fortune Business Insights, "Pet Supplements Market" report, and Grand View Research, "Pet Supplements Market" report (2026 editions; range reflects differing category scope).
- **Funding/M&A:** AlphaPet Ventures acquired German pet-supplement/functional-snack brand Tierliebhaber on April 1, 2026 — its sixth acquisition since 2020 and second of Q1 2026, financed with equity/debt led by Patria Investments. — GÖRG law firm deal announcement / EQS News, April 1, 2026. Separately, FoodScience (parent of VetriScience, DaVinci Labs, and Pet Naturals) has been folded into Morgan Stanley Capital Partners' portfolio, continuing PE consolidation in the category. — dvm360 coverage.
- **Brands to watch:** PetLab Co. — a fast-growing, DTC-and-Amazon-first supplement brand (chews, skin/coat, joint support) that took majority PE backing from BC Partners; still one of the most-cited scale stories in pet supplements. FoodScience/VetriScience — now PE-backed and consolidating smaller wellness brands under one roof, a trend worth watching for competitive pressure on independent supplement sellers.

## 3. Toys & Enrichment

- **Market size/growth:** The global dog toys market is estimated at $4.42 billion in 2026, heading toward $7.67 billion by 2034 (7.12% CAGR); the U.S. dog toys market specifically is projected to grow from $4,785.3 million (2026) to $6,587.1 million by 2035 (3.5% CAGR). Chew toys are expected to hold ~36% of global product-type demand in 2026. — Fortune Business Insights and Future Market Insights, "Pet/Dog Toys Market" reports (2026 editions).
- **Funding/M&A:** SportPet Designs acquired Mammoth Pet Products, a California-based premium dog-toy maker, in January 2026 — part of a broader wave of strategic (not PE) toy-category consolidation flagged as a 2026 trend. — Pet Age, "Pet Industry Mergers & Acquisitions: A Look Back at 2025, What's Ahead for 2026."
- **Brand to watch:** WAGLAB — a new toy-innovation brand from the team behind Canada Pooch, launched its debut "PlayStack" modular/interchangeable toy system on May 4, 2026 (Pet Valu Canada) with a broader U.S./independent-retailer rollout in June 2026; notable for a build-your-own, five-piece interchangeable toy system rather than a single fixed product. — PR Newswire, April 27, 2026.

## 4. Food & Treats

- **Market size/growth:** The pet treats market is on pace to grow from roughly $20.1 billion (2021) to $29.7 billion by 2031 (4.2% CAGR), with dogs holding the largest treats-segment share (~52.1%) in 2026 and dental-health treats projected to post the category's fastest growth. — MarketsandMarkets, "Pet Treats Market Report" (2026–2031 edition). Fresh dog food remains the fastest-growing sub-segment: category leader Freshpet passed $1 billion in annual net sales in 2025, now sells through 30,000+ stores, and guided to 7–10% net-sales growth for 2026. — Pet Food Processing.
- **Funding/M&A:** Golden Child, a Miami-based "science-backed" fresh dog food startup co-founded by former Hims & Hers exec Jack Abraham, emerged from stealth on April 28, 2026 with $37 million in funding from Atomic, A*, and Redpoint Ventures. — TechCrunch / BusinessWire, April 27–28, 2026. Around the same window, ultra-premium entrant Drizzle launched targeting affluent dog owners. — TechCrunch, April 27, 2026.
- **Brands to watch:** Golden Child (well-capitalized new entrant explicitly positioned against nutrition gaps in existing premium fresh brands); Drizzle (targeting the top of the market with "human-grade" positioning) — both signal continued investor appetite for premium/fresh dog food specifically, even as overall pet-food M&A stays selective.

## 5. Accessories (Collars/Leashes/Carriers/Beds)

- **Market size/growth:** The U.S. pet accessories market is estimated at roughly $14.97 billion in 2026 (up from ~$14 billion in 2025), heading toward $25.59 billion by 2034 (6.93% CAGR). — MarketDataForecast, "United States Pet Accessories Market" report. The narrower global dog collars/leashes/harnesses market is estimated at $6.8 billion in 2026, growing to $12.1 billion by 2033 (8.6% CAGR), driven by premiumization and "quiet luxury" positioning. — Persistence Market Research, "Dog Collars, Leashes & Harnesses Market" report.
- **Funding/M&A:** Paw Prosper Inc. acquired travel-safety brand Sleepypod (known for its crash-tested Mobile Pet Bed/carrier hybrid) — deal reported January 2025, the most recent identifiable accessories-category acquisition; no newer (2026) accessories M&A surfaced despite broadened searches.
- **Brands to watch:** Lesure — a pet-lifestyle brand backed by human-mattress maker Bedshe International, which launched the "RealOrtho" orthopedic dog bed built on human sleep science (announced July 22, 2026), notable for applying human bedding R&D directly to a dog product. Sleepypod — expanded its car-safety line with the "Clickit" harness range (launched January 2026), engineered to fit a broader range of canine body types, reinforcing the safety-tech angle within accessories.

## 6. Tech & Gadgets (GPS Trackers, Smart Feeders, etc.)

- **Market size/growth:** Pet wearables are now a $3.8 billion industry in 2026, projected to reach $11.4 billion by 2033. The GPS-tracker smart-collar sub-segment specifically is estimated at $0.72 billion in 2026, growing to $1.64 billion by 2035 (9.4% CAGR); smart feeders are the fastest-growing pet-tech sub-segment, projected at a 12.8% CAGR from 2026–2033. — Fortune, July 8, 2026, and Business Research Insights, "GPS Tracker Smart Dog Collar Market" report (2026 edition).
- **Funding/M&A:** Fi (GPS dog collar) announced a partnership bringing SpaceX Starlink direct-to-cell satellite connectivity to its collars — reported exclusively July 8, 2026. Fi has raised $45 million total (Series B led by Longview Asset Management), has quietly expanded to 38 countries, and expects to cross $100 million ARR in 2026. — Fortune, July 8, 2026. Separately, robotic-companion startup Tombot closed a $7 million Series A3 round on June 25, 2026 to scale manufacturing of "Jennie," its lifelike robotic Labrador puppy, ahead of a Fall 2026 launch; the company has 23,000+ pre-order/waitlist customers. — BusinessWire, June 25, 2026.
- **Brands to watch:** Fi — pushing the category's technical frontier with satellite (not just cellular/Bluetooth) connectivity, a meaningful differentiator versus rival Tractive. Tombot — a genuinely novel "tech meets companionship" product (robotic dog) that's pre-selling well ahead of launch, worth watching as a signal of where premium pet-tech demand is heading.
