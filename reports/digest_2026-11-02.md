## Top signals this week
- **i-Tail's $160M acquisition of Phelps Pet Products** (closed Oct 22, 2026) is the biggest concrete move this week — a major pet-care conglomerate buying US jerky-treat manufacturing capacity outright, signaling continued consolidation pressure in the treats category that a handmade-treat Etsy seller competes adjacent to.
- **TikTok Shop is a real, growing sales channel for dog products**, not just a discovery surface: UK pet-care sales up 60% YoY, one pet product sold every 15 seconds, and at least one brand (Herbal Dog Co.) reportedly doing ~$10,000/day in live-session sales. Worth testing as a channel alongside Etsy.
- **US pet industry spend is growing (~$165B projected for 2026, +4.4%) but owners are value-seeking** — 22% of pet owners report spending less in 2025 than 2024 (APPA). For a small-batch seller, this argues for clear value framing, not necessarily discounting.
- **Thule Group's ~$13M acquisition of Curli AG** (premium Swiss dog-harness maker, closing Q2 2026) is a strategic-buyer signal that premium dog accessories/harnesses are attractive enough for a major outdoor-gear company to buy into — a category validation data point for anyone selling premium handmade accessories.
- **Sustained, low-cost-to-replicate product trends on social**: snuffle balls and suction-cup tug toys continue as recurring viral/sustained TikTok favorites in the enrichment-toy space — both are plausible categories for a small-batch maker to enter without large capital.
