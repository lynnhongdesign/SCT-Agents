### 2. Supplements & Wellness
- Market estimates range $2.62B–$5.11B in 2026 depending on research firm; pet CBD segment specifically ~$576M in 2026 (MarkWide Research).
- Funding activity: **PetPax Co.** won Purina's 10th annual Pet Care Innovation Prize ($25K, Mar 26 2026); **Omni Pet** raised £11M (~$14.5M) Series A (Jun 2026) for a plant-based "Ozempic for dogs" supplement; **Kradle** closed a $4M Series 3A; Canadian **Pawsible Ventures** launched a CAD $10M pet-wellness fund.
- Brands to watch: **PetPax Co.** (Purina-validated, differentiated thin-film format) and **Omni Pet** (well-funded, novel GLP-1-adjacent positioning).

### 3. Toys & Enrichment
- Global pet toys market ~$3.79B–$10.0B in 2026 depending on scope; dogs hold ~67.57% category share. Durable/indestructible toy sub-segment ~$1.8B.
- **H.I.G. Capital acquired Outward Hound** (toys/treats/accessories portfolio) in a reported July 2026 deal — the standout M&A signal for this category.
- Brand to watch: **SodaPup**, credited with helping define the modern food-enrichment toy category (eChews/eCoins lines). No second toy-specific brand could be confirmed within the recency window.

### 4. Food & Treats
- US/global dog food market projected $80.34B (2026) → $117.22B by 2034 (4.84% CAGR). Pet treats market $32.9B–$38.4B in 2026 depending on firm.
- **Golden Child** (Miami fresh dog food, co-founded by Jack Abraham of Hims & Hers) raised $37M from Atomic, a16z's A*, and Redpoint (Apr 2026); **Agrolimen acquired Ollie** for over $600M (Feb 2026); **Assisi Pet Care acquired Sniffers Pet Care** (owner of NAW treats); **Nom Nom** (Mars) debuted Scoopable Meal Clusters at SuperZoo 2026.
- Brands to watch: **Golden Child** (well-capitalized new entrant) and **Nom Nom** (continued format innovation from an established player).

### 5. Accessories (collars/leashes/carriers/beds)
- Collars/leashes/harnesses market ~$6.8B in 2026 (8.6% CAGR to $12.1B by 2033); dog bed market ~$9.18B in 2026 (8.58% CAGR); pet furniture broadly ~$7.95B, with sofas/beds ~38.2% share.

- Brands to watch: **Dogistry** (fashion-forward premium essentials) and **Caramel & cie** (French fashion-house entrant into pet carriers using recycled polyester).

### 6. Tech & Gadgets (GPS trackers, smart feeders, etc.)
