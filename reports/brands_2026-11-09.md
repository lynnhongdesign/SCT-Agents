- **[Brand move]** BARK, Inc. (BarkBox parent) reported fiscal Q2 FY2027 results (quarter ended Sept. 30, 2026): net revenue of $87.6M, beating its own guidance range of $83.0M–$85.0M. Subscriber retention improved to 94%, and CEO Matt Meeker said the Commerce segment "returned to growth." Separately, the company disclosed a preliminary agreement to sell BARK Air's assets and license the BARK Air brand starting Jan. 1, 2027 (no dollar figure disclosed; deal not yet finalized). Full-year Adjusted EBITDA guidance set at $7M–$10M. (Source: BARK Inc. press release/8-K, via Barchart/Finviz aggregation, published Nov. 5, 2026.)

- **[Brand move]** Freshpet posted Q3 2026 results (quarter ended Sept. 30, reported Nov. 4, 2026): net sales rose 10.0% YoY to $317.8M (from $288.8M), driven almost entirely by volume gains rather than pricing. Adjusted gross margin hit 50%, its strongest in over seven years (up from 46%), and e-commerce sales grew 35% YoY, now 16.9% of total sales. Management raised full-year 2026 net sales growth guidance to 12–13% (from 10–12%). (Source: Freshpet Inc. Q3 2026 earnings release, via Petfood Industry/247wallst.com aggregation, published Nov. 4, 2026.)

- **[Brand move]** Nulo expanded into Hollywood Feed's full store footprint — 179 locations across 19 states — stocking its grain-free Freestyle line, oven-baked Culinary Harvest recipes, and wet foods. A soft launch began Oct. 19 in select Georgia, Indiana and Ohio markets, with full rollout to all stores starting Nov. 2, 2026. Nulo's COO Greg Aldrich specifically praised the retailer's in-store customer education as the rationale for the deepened partnership. (Source: Petfood Industry / Petfood Processing, published Oct. 23, 2026.)

- **[Brand move]** K9 Natural brought its "Baked & Coated" dry dog food line into Canada exclusively through Pet Valu (and sister banners Bosley's, Paulmac's Pets, Tisol, Total Pet), with exclusivity running through May 2027 — the brand's first dry/kibble product in that market. Three recipes launched (Grass-Fed Red, Cage-Free Poultry, Wild Ocean Fish), building on a September 2025 partnership that had brought K9 Natural's freeze-dried/canned lines into Pet Valu stores. (Source: Petfood Industry, published Oct. 28, 2026.)
