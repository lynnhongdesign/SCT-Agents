- **Market size:** Global pet accessories market estimated at **$7.6B in 2026 growing to $12.3B by 2033 (7.1% CAGR)** per one report, though a broader-scope estimate puts it at **$43.34B in 2026 → $76.4B by 2035 (6.5% CAGR)**.
