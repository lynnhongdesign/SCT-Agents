## 1. Apparel & Wear
- Global pet clothing market estimated at ~$6.31B in 2026 (6.31% CAGR to 2034), per Fortune Business Insights (snippet only, report page undated but 2026 edition). Dog-specific sub-estimates vary enormously by publisher (from ~$1.2B to ~$8.7B for 2026), so treat any single dog-only figure with caution.
- No apparel-specific funding or M&A activity surfaced in this search round — explicitly nothing fresh found.
- Brands to watch: **Spark Paws** — built a reported ~$16M pet-apparel brand after its founders (French Bulldog owners) couldn't find clothing fitted for bulldogs' broad chests/thick necks; matching human-and-dog sets are a bestseller (socialbook.io, snippet only, undated — verify independently). **Bar Dog** — leash/collar brand from creator Halley Kate Williams (2M+ TikTok/Instagram following) relaunched July 30, 2026 with a full apparel/knitwear line, an example of creator-led brands expanding into apparel (snippet only).

## 2. Supplements & Wellness
- US pet supplements market sized at $2.9B with a 3% CAGR forecast for 2026–2030, per Packaged Facts/Freedonia Group's 11th edition "Pet Supplements in the US" report (snippet only, 2026 edition). A separate Pet Food Industry piece ("Report: Pet supplement sales near $3B as growth moderates") corroborates the sub-$3B US figure but growth is described as moderating (snippet only, date not confirmed).
- Funding: **Omni Pet** (UK, dog weight-loss/wellness supplements marketed as an "Ozempic-style" product) raised £11M (~$14.5M), reported June 23, 2026, after a year of roughly tenfold revenue growth (Vegconomist/The Grocer, snippet only). **Oh Norman!** (Kaley Cuoco co-founded dog wellness brand) closed a $2.08M round, oversubscribed 39% against a $1.5M target, with Mars Petcare among investors (Pet Food Industry, snippet only — exact announcement date not confirmed in snippet).
- Brands to watch: Omni Pet (fastest-growing in this set, tenfold YoY growth cited) and Oh Norman! (celebrity-backed, oversubscribed raise, strategic Mars investor interest).

## 3. Toys & Enrichment
- Total US/global pet toys market estimated at $11.6B in 2026 growing to $22.4B by 2033 (9.9% CAGR), per Grand View Research (snippet only). The narrower interactive/enrichment dog-toy sub-segment is estimated at roughly $1.18B in 2026 (7.33% CAGR) by one 360iResearch listing, though a competing estimate puts the same niche near $126–292M — definitions vary widely between publishers, so treat any single enrichment-only number cautiously.
- No toy-brand-specific funding or M&A found this round. Closest adjacent deal: **Tombot** (AI robotic companion dog, arguably therapeutic-enrichment product) closed a Series A3 growth round reported at ~$7M, aiming for a Fall 2026 consumer launch (TheNextWeb, snippet only, article dated June 25, 2026) — explicitly flagging this is not a pure toy-category raise.
- Brands to watch: **SodaPup** — started as a single rubber treat-dispenser in 2013, now positioning food-enrichment puzzles as mainstream rather than niche (per company statement, snippet only, undated). **Tombot** — notable for strong VC interest in an "unplugged"/low-tech-adjacent robotic pet companion ahead of a 2026 launch.

## 4. Food & Treats
- US dog treat sales reached ~$7.3B in 2026, up 2.4% YoY (cat treats grew faster, +12.5% to $2.3B), per Pet Food Processing's "State of the Pet Food/Treat Industry in 2026" (snippet only). Freeze-dried dog food is a standout sub-trend, up 17.3% to $416.6M (same source, snippet only). Broader context: total US pet industry spend hit $158B in 2025 per APPA's State of the Industry report (APPA/Pet Food Industry, dated March 26, 2026, snippet only), with food & treats the largest spending category.
- M&A: The most recent notable dog-food deal — **Agrolimen (Spain, via Affinity Petcare) acquired fresh dog-food brand Ollie**, reported ~$600M+ valuation (unconfirmed by either party); announced via CEO LinkedIn post and Reuters coverage dated February 6–7, 2026, broader trade coverage February 9, 2026 (Pet Age, Reuters, Global Pet Industry — snippet only). This is 9+ months old, not within the 14-day window — explicitly noting no fresher dog-food M&A surfaced this run.
- Brands to watch: **Ollie** (fresh dog food, just acquired at a premium valuation — signals strong strategic/category interest in premium fresh format). **Happy Howl** (US DTC shelf-stable, gently-cooked dog food) received an investment from Mars Petcare to fund its "next phase of growth and product innovation" (Pet Food Industry, snippet only, exact date unconfirmed).

## 5. Accessories (collars/leashes/carriers/beds)
- Dog collars/leashes/harnesses market estimated at ~$6.8B in 2026 (6.4% CAGR through 2036) — note this figure traces to a manufacturer-linked press release (OKEYPETS), so treat as directional only. Pet carriers market more reliably sized at $936.95M in 2026 by Fortune Business Insights (6.60% CAGR to 2034, snippet only). Pet beds estimated at $5.84B (2025) rising to $8.85B by 2032 (6.12% CAGR) per Persistence Market Research (snippet only).
- No accessories-specific funding or M&A activity surfaced this round — explicitly nothing fresh found.
- Brands to watch: **Gentle Wuff** — newer entrant launching a full harness/collar/leash/accessories line, positioned on "performance meets style" (Pet Insight, snippet only, dated November 6, 2025 — just outside this week but recent-vintage launch). **Wild One** — established (2018) but still cited repeatedly as the benchmark minimalist, function-first premium accessories label driving category positioning (Maker's Row/Hepper, snippet only).

## 6. Tech & Gadgets (GPS trackers, smart feeders, etc.)
- Pet GPS tracking device market estimated at $1.88B in 2026, growing to $4.42B by 2033 (13.01% CAGR) per DIResearch (snippet only). Broader smart pet wearables estimated between $3.4B–$4.8B for 2026 depending on publisher (Polaris Market Research, MarkWide, Grand View Research all disagree — wide variance, flagged for caution).
- Funding: **Lassie** (dog health/insurance-adjacent tech) closed a $75M Series C in February 2026, reportedly accounting for ~93% of all pet-tech venture capital raised in 2026 to date (Seedtable-sourced ranking, snippet only) — the dominant pet-tech funding event of the year so far. Secondary/adjacent: **BistroCat** smart feeder (cat-focused, included for context) raised a ~$2.06M Series A dated July 8, 2026 (PitchBook, snippet only).
- Brands to watch: **Halo Collar** — installation-free GPS smart-fencing/training collar reportedly at ~$100M in sales, driven by patent strategy and expert partnerships (snippet only, exact date unconfirmed). **Fi** — smart GPS/cellular dog collar (founded 2017) repeatedly named across multiple 2026 "startups to watch" lists for sustained consumer traction (Startup Savant, snippet only).
