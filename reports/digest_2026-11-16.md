## Top Signals This Week
- **i-Tail (Thai Union) completed its $160M acquisition of Phelps Pet Products** (~2.3x Phelps's 2025 net sales of $70.3M), expanding US pet-treat manufacturing — the largest confirmed M&A move across any category this week. (SeafoodSource/Pet Food Industry, Oct 26–Nov 4 2026, snippet only)
- **Freshpet's Q3 2026 results show growth decelerating** — net sales +10% to $317.8M, down from +15.5% in Q2 and +13.1% in Q1, though margins improved (44.5% gross margin vs. 39.5% a year ago) and FY guidance was raised to 12-13%. Worth watching as a bellwether for fresh/premium dog food demand. (Pet Food Industry, Nov 4 2026, snippet only)
- **"Iced-out" crystal dog chain collars are a real, sustained TikTok Shop trend**, not a one-off — a top Cuban-link-style listing shows 24.5K sold, and dog apparel is reportedly TikTok Shop's fastest-growing pet subcategory (+34.75% since September 2026). This is the most directly actionable signal for a handmade/small-batch Etsy seller this week. (TikTok Shop listings / tt123.com tracker, Sept–Nov 2026, snippet only)
- **Tier-1 VC money is flowing into fresh/functional dog food, not treats or toys**: Golden Child raised $37M (Atomic, A*, Redpoint) for vet-science fresh food; DASH Dog Food raised a $1.5M oversubscribed seed and dropped dry food entirely to focus on fresh-frozen. Capital is chasing premiumization in food specifically. (Pulse2/VCA Online, Apr–Jun 2026, snippet only)
- **Connected pet-tech consolidation is underway**: Bending Spoons (a serial subscription-app acquirer) is buying GPS tracker maker Tractive, while PitPat ($4M Series A) and Traini ($7.5M, AI emotion-sensing collar) both raised fresh rounds in the same window — signals investors see durable subscription revenue in pet wearables. (Multiple trade press, Feb–Mar 2026, snippet only)
