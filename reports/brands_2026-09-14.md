# Agent 3 — Brands & Companies
### Week of 2026-09-14

**Note:** All primary trade sources (petfoodindustry.com, petage.com, petfoodprocessing.net, prnewswire.com, aol.com) returned network-blocked errors on WebFetch this session — every item below is sourced from WebSearch result snippets and cross-referenced across 2-3 outlets, not full-article fetches.

- **[Brand move] The Farmer's Dog named NFL's first-ever Official Pet Food Partner** — announced September 9, 2026. Multi-year, multi-market deal makes the human-grade fresh dog food company the league's first pet-food partner, with a launch campaign fronted by NY Giants RB Cam Skattebo and his senior rescue dog, Duke. Activations extend to Super Bowl LXI, the NFL Combine, NFL Draft, My Cause My Cleats, and NFL Salute to Service; builds on the brand's existing Dallas Cowboys sponsorship and its June 2026 NASCAR tie-up with Ryan Blaney. Signals continued heavy marketing investment by a well-funded DTC dog food player to build mainstream household-name status. (Athlon Sports, PRNewswire, via WebSearch snippets — full pages blocked)

- **[Brand move] Girl Scouts of the USA x BARK launch "Patch Pals," first-ever dog cookie** — unveiled September 8, 2026, ahead of the 2027 cookie season. Developed with BARK (of BarkBox), it's a first for the 100+ year-old cookie program and will be sold online-exclusive, shipping alongside the traditional human lineup (Thin Mints, Samoas, etc.). Notable as a legacy consumer brand entering the dog treat category via partnership rather than acquisition — a low-risk expansion-into-new-channel play for BARK to reach a mainstream, non-pet-specific audience. (Washington Post, Today.com, Girl Scouts press room, via WebSearch snippets)

- **[Brand move] Barkhouse launches NYC's first-ever "Dog Fashion Week"** — ran September 8-12, 2026, in Chelsea. Hosted by Barkhouse (an "elevated dog daycare" membership concept open only nine months, ~700 users), the week featured daily pop-ups from four dog fashion/accessory brands (Little Beast, By Teddy, Pawelier, 1ER) culminating in a 50-foot runway show presented by Fetch Pet Insurance and Maev. Notable as a small, young dog-hospitality startup using an experiential/brand-partnership format to build community and drive membership — a channel-expansion model relevant to niche/small-batch sellers thinking beyond e-commerce. (Fashion United, PetGuide, The Underbite, via WebSearch snippets)

- **[Brand move] Chewy reports Q2 FY2026 results — sales and profitability both up** — released September 9, 2026, for the quarter ended August 2, 2026. Net sales rose 7.3% YoY to $3.33B (high end of guidance); net income $80.5M (2.4% margin, +40bps YoY); Adjusted EBITDA $226.7M (+$43.4M YoY); active customers up 3.8% to 21.7M; Autoship sales climbed 9.3% to $2.8B, now 84.6% of total net sales — underscoring how subscription/repeat-purchase mechanics keep driving growth for the largest pet e-commerce channel independent sellers compete alongside. (Chewy investor relations, StockTitan, MarketBeat, via WebSearch snippets)

- **[Brand move] The Farmer's Dog agrees to acquire Woof, expanding beyond food into enrichment/wellness** — announced August 6, 2026 (slightly outside the 14-day window but still closing/relevant context for the NFL deal above). Definitive agreement to acquire the refillable dog-wellness-device brand (maker of the "Pupsicle"); terms undisclosed, deal expected to close later in 2026, Woof to run as an independent division. Shows the category leader diversifying from pure nutrition into adjacent enrichment products. (Pulse2, Feed & Additive Magazine, via WebSearch snippets)
