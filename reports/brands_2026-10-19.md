**[Brand move] Pets at Home — £110m store-estate overhaul, 460 locations (UK)**
Source: Retail Gazette / market coverage, Oct 15, 2026 (snippet only — full page blocked)
The UK's largest pet retailer is putting over £110 million into refreshing 460 of its stores under its "Our Space Reset" program, part of a broader Retail Turnaround Plan. COO Anja Madsen said roughly five stores are being reset per week, with new layouts carving out more floor space for growth categories (grooming, vet services, premium food) versus commodity SKUs. The same coverage notes Pets at Home has grown its Active Pets Club loyalty membership to 7 million and cut £20 million in overhead — framing this as a profitability push, not just a cosmetic refresh. Relevant to sellers watching where big-box retail is allocating shelf space next.

**[Brand move] The Canine Project launches FURVIVE all-in-one dog shampoo**
Source: Global Cosmetic Industry (trade press), Oct 15, 2026
The Canine Project, a dog beauty/skincare brand co-founded by skincare formulator Jan Sprung and Nancy Novograd (owner of All Tame Animals talent agency), launched FURVIVE Restorative Shampoo + Conditioner + Body Wash. Notable specifics: it's built around a proprietary "COPAIBOPLEX" blend using Brazilian copaiba resin (a novel ingredient in dog skincare) plus niacinamide, hyaluronic acid, panthenol, and shea/argan oils; the formula starts as a concentrated gel that stays put on application, then transforms into a lather. Vegan, pH-balanced for canine skin, priced ~$33/12 fl oz. A good comp for small-batch sellers: premium ingredient storytelling (one "hero" botanical) as the entire launch hook.

**[Brand move] DASH Dog Food closes oversubscribed $1.5M seed round**
Source: FinSMEs / National Law Review press release / GlobalPETS roundup, round closed Sept 22, 2026 (covered in roundups through Oct 6, 2026)
DASH, a fresh-frozen "table-grade" dog food brand founded by Bobby Quirk (ButcherBox's first hire) after spinning out of ButcherBox in June 2025, closed a $1.5M seed round led by Voltage Ventures (animal-health focused), with Western Technology Investment and individual investors participating. Voltage's Marissa Bertorelli and ButcherBox founder Mike Salguero joined the board. Stated use of funds: expanding paid customer acquisition and the subscription model, plus ~10% earmarked for R&D on two new proteins (seafood, turkey) and a packaging redesign. Company claims 130% revenue growth since splitting from ButcherBox, after dropping a dry-food line in a June 2026 rebrand to focus solely on fresh-frozen.

**[Brand move] The Farmer's Dog completes acquisition of Woof, its first-ever M&A deal**
Source: Petfood Industry / Signalbase (snippet only — full page blocked), deal announced Aug 2026, completion reported Sept 19, 2026
The Farmer's Dog — the well-funded fresh dog food subscription company — completed its acquisition of Woof, a pet wellness brand, marking the company's first acquisition since founding. Woof will continue operating as an independent division rather than being folded into The Farmer's Dog's core brand. Stated rationale: expansion beyond food into enrichment/wellness categories. Financial terms were not disclosed. Worth watching as a signal that a major DTC pet-food player is building a multi-brand portfolio rather than staying single-category.

**[Brand move] The Farmer's Dog becomes NFL's first-ever official pet food partner**
Source: DVM360, Sept 9, 2026
The Farmer's Dog signed a multiyear partnership making it the NFL's first official pet food category partner. The deal includes activations tied to Super Bowl LXI, the NFL Combine, and the NFL Draft. This is a mainstream-sports brand play uncommon for a company that built its name on vet-endorsed, direct-to-consumer subscription food — a notable channel/visibility expansion beyond typical pet-media placements.
