## Top Signals This Week

1. **The Farmer's Dog acquired Woof (closed Sept 18, 2026)** — its first-ever acquisition, pushing the fresh-food giant into enrichment/wellness products (Woof makes refillable items like the "Pupsicle" and was #3 on the 2025 Inc. 5000 list). This is the single best-corroborated item this week, cross-confirmed by both the Macro Stats and Brands agents. Signals that adjacent-category expansion (food brand → wellness/enrichment) is an active playbook among larger players.

2. **DASH Dog Food raised a $1.5M seed round led by Voltage Ventures** (~Sept 21-22, 2026) for fresh-frozen dog food, founded by a ButcherBox alum applying human-food cold-chain sourcing standards. Oversubscribed round; funds earmarked for subscription growth and new recipes (seafood, turkey).

3. **Wuffes launched a Dental Powder line (oral care, "SuperYolk IgY" egg-yolk antibody tech)** — notable not just as a product launch but because Wuffes reports 3-year revenue growth over 1,000% and recently landed PetSmart and Tractor Supply shelf space (Sept 22, 2026). A concrete example of a DTC-born brand successfully bridging into big-box retail.

4. **Joyride's Halloween glow-in-the-dark harness/leash collection is selling out in limited-run colorways** (Black Skulls leash sold out by Sept 21, 2026). Directly relevant to a small-batch Etsy seller: it's evidence that limited-run, seasonal, scarcity-marketed pet apparel is working right now — a model replicable at small scale.

5. **Supplement-category funding stayed active**: Native Pet ($11M Series B, CAVU Consumer Partners) and Other Half Pet ($3M seed, Willow Growth) both raised rounds in the Supplements & Wellness category, reinforcing that wellness/supplements remains one of the more fundable sub-categories in the space right now.
