### Findings

- **Cheerble Wicked Ball Air (self-propelling interactive ball toy)** — *Sustained trend.* Source type: General search (brand press releases + Amazon marketplace listings + independent gadget review + ongoing creator-seeding campaigns). The manufacturer's own March 2025 press release claims it jumped into the top 3 Amazon Best-Seller Rank in its category and calls it "one of the most talked about pet toys of 2024." Separately, Cheerble has been running paid/gifted TikTok+Instagram+Facebook creator-seeding campaigns (job listings dated Dec 2024 and again in 2025) specifically recruiting dog-account creators to film the toy, which explains sustained multi-creator video volume into 2025–2026 rather than a single moment. Caveat: the "viral" framing is largely brand-sourced, not independently verified view counts.

- **Snuffle mats (nose-work/enrichment feeding mats)** — *Sustained trend.* Source type: General search (recurring across "TikTok made me buy" roundup articles spanning multiple years, e.g. iHeartDogs, Benable creator lists, Kinship) plus third-party Amazon sales-trend reports. AS Insight's June 2026 report tracks 122 "snuffle mat for large dogs" products with the top ASIN estimated at ~9K monthly units; its February 2026 "snuffle mat for dogs" report (1,149 tracked products) shows continued category activity. The product recurs in essentially every TikTok-pet-product listicle found across 2024–2026, which is the defining signature of a sustained trend rather than a single spike.

- **Potaroma Electric Flopping Fish toy (motion-sensor flopping fish, marketed as a cat toy but adopted heavily by dog owners)** — *Single viral moment.* Source type: Press coverage (AOL/In The Know syndicated lifestyle articles, plus a separate Yahoo/AOL "majorly on sale" follow-up piece). Coverage frames it as a toy "discovered this year" by TikTok users who shared videos of dogs leaping and pouncing on it; one write-up specifically credits "the most influential dogs of TikTok" with popularizing it even though it's nominally a cat product. No verifiable publish date or view-count metric was recoverable from search snippets (WebFetch to pull the actual byline/date failed), so treat the recency claim with some caution — this reads as a recurring seasonal sales-push story rather than a freshly-dated Oct 2026 event.

- **Purina Beggin' Bat Bites (bacon-flavored, bat-shaped Halloween dog treats, Target-exclusive)** — *Single viral moment, seasonal.* Source type: Press coverage (trade press — petfoodindustry.com / massmarketretailers.com — plus an AOL lifestyle piece on Target's companion "ghost-shaped" treats). Concrete figure: Target's own product listing states "more than 20,000 bought in the past month." This is current-season, Halloween-specific inventory ($3.69/6oz pouch, Target-exclusive, "while supplies last"), which lines up tightly with the Oct 19, 2026 report date. Weaker than the above two on "social media" virality specifically (the sourcing is retail/trade press reporting a sales figure, not a documented hashtag or creator wave), but it's the most concretely dated and numerically-backed finding from this research pass.

**Note on gaps:** A related seasonal item — Target's Bark-brand "bacon-scented bat" chew toy and "Gingergnaw Man" holiday chew toy — turned up in search with "going viral" headlines, but no recoverable publish date and overlapping older syndicated copy (the Gingergnaw piece appears to echo prior-season holiday-toy coverage), so it was excluded in favor of the better-dated Beggin' Bat Bites item above.
