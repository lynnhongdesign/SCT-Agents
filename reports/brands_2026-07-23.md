## Weekly Market Intelligence Brief: Dog Product Brands & Companies (Week of July 23, 2026)

*Note on sourcing: The tracked hub (petfoodindustry.com) and several other trade/press domains (petage.com, business-sale.com, globenewswire.com, openpr.com) returned HTTP 403 on direct WebFetch — confirmed as network/proxy-level policy denials via `/__agentproxy/status`, not per-source errors. Compensated with 8 varied WebSearch queries, cross-checking each finding against 2-3 independent outlets and verifying publish dates before inclusion. All items below fall in the July 14-21, 2026 window.*

- **[Brand move]** Assisi Pet Care (owner of Burns, Hollings, HiLife, Pet Munchies, Yakers, and other brands) acquired Devon-based natural dog food maker **Forthglade** from IK Partners, per petfoodindustry.com and multiple UK trade outlets (July 20-21, 2026). Terms undisclosed, but Forthglade's financials were reported: £39.5m turnover for the year to Sept 30, 2025, with EBITDA up 26.8% to £4.7m. The deal is Assisi's latest buy since Wind Point Partners' 2023 acquisition of the group, aimed at building a "leading European platform in premium, natural pet care" — Forthglade brings strength across grocery, specialist, and online channels.

- **[Brand move]** Royal Canin (Mars) launched **Royal Canin Fresh Veterinary Diets** in the U.S. — its first-ever fresh, fully-cooked therapeutic diet line, sold exclusively through veterinarians and vet online pharmacy partners (announced July 14, 2026, per PR Newswire/Mars/petfoodindustry.com). The portfolio launches with three formulas: Gastrointestinal Low Fat, Selected Protein Pork (single-protein for food sensitivities), and Appetite Stimulation (for dogs recovering from illness or weight loss) — signaling a major legacy player pushing further into the fresh-food category that smaller DTC brands pioneered.

- **[Brand move]** **BetterBone by Blue Standard** (eco-friendly dog chews) announced a global distribution expansion, adding new market partners in India, French Canada (via Freedom Pet and Pan Pacific Pet), China, and Brazil (reported July 20, 2026, Pet Age). This builds on existing distribution in the UK, South Korea, Canada, Mexico, Venezuela, Indonesia, and Singapore, pushing the brand's retail footprint past thousands of locations across 26 countries — a channel-expansion play by a mid-size sustainability-focused chew brand.

- **[Brand move]** New entrant **Paws & Whiskers**, a vet-formulated dog supplement startup, officially launched in the U.S. (July 17, 2026, per press coverage on OpenPR and syndicated outlets). Founded after co-founder Matt's experience caring for his German Shepherd through liver cancer and arthritis, the line — covering mobility, digestion, joint health, and everyday wellness — was developed with veterinarian Dr. Petar Petrov (20+ years in animal nutrition) and is made in FDA-registered U.S. facilities, free of soy, corn, and artificial preservatives/flavors — an example of a small, founder-story-driven brand entering a crowded supplement space.
