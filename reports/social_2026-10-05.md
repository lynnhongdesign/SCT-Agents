# Agent 2 — Social Media Product Trends (Oct 5, 2026)

**Research process note:** All direct WebFetch attempts in this session — against Reddit (www.reddit.com and old.reddit.com, both r/dogs, r/DogAdvice, and r/dogtraining top.json/weekly endpoints), TikTok.com, Instagram-linked pages, and several news/blog domains (apartmenttherapy.com, findniche.com, netinfluencer.com, hauntedattractionnetwork.com, woofgangbakery.com, abcnews.go.com, even en.wikipedia.org as a control test) — were blocked at the network level by this environment's egress proxy (`EGRESS_BLOCKED` or generic "unable to fetch" errors), not by the target sites themselves. This affected Tracked Sources (source type 1) and Direct Platform Page fetches (source type 2) entirely — raw Reddit JSON and TikTok/Instagram post HTML could not be pulled directly. This was compensated for by running 9+ varied WebSearch queries across site-restricted searches, platform-trend-coverage searches, and general searches (source types 2–4), which return pre-fetched/summarized snippets from the search backend itself. Findings below rely on those snippets, cross-checked for specific dates/numbers where possible; anything that couldn't be date-verified (e.g., an iHeartDogs "everywhere in 2026" roundup, a Pet Portrait Treat Box Etsy anecdote, a recycled 2023/2024 NRF $700M stat) was dropped rather than included on faith.

---

- **Joyride Halloween glow-in-the-dark dog harness collection** — [Sustained trend] — [Press coverage]
  Direct-to-consumer harness brand Joyride launched a 2026 Halloween line (Black Skulls, Pink Skulls, plus limited-run Fossil Frenzy, Midnight Web, Bat Season patterns) — harnesses $59.95–$69.95, matching leashes $24.95, with full glow-in-the-dark reflective panels and free customizable name tags on limited patterns. Small-batch runs are selling out (Black Skulls leash was already sold out on-site as of September 21, 2026). Source: Haunted Attraction Network, "Dog Halloween Is Having a Moment in 2026, From Glow Harnesses to Viral Ghost Dogs" (content dated via internal sell-out evidence to ~Sept 21, 2026, within the 14-day window).

- **Home Accents Holiday LED "Ghost Dog" yard decoration (Home Depot)** — [Single viral moment] — [Press coverage + General search]
  A real, buyable $40 2.5-ft battery-operated LED ghost-dog yard stake (Golden Retriever shape, with breed variants — Labrador, Frenchie, Doodle, Terrier — sold as "Puppy Greeter" at $44.98, and a Skeleton Costume variant at $79.98) has spun off a TikTok customization trend: creator @morganzuhmakes repainted/transformed the stock decoration into an Australian Shepherd-styled ghost dog, a video cited at ~6 million views. This is distinct from the broader non-product "#ghosttrend" (dogs under bedsheets, 279M+ views) which was excluded as pure entertainment with no product at its center. Source: same Haunted Attraction Network article as above (~Sept 21, 2026).

- **"Build a Box – Natural Dog Treats – Build Your Own Bundle" (TikTok Shop UK)** — [Sustained trend] — [General search / platform-sales-tracker data]
  A customizable/personalizable dog-treat bundle is the #1 ranked pet-supplies product on TikTok Shop UK per FindNiche's trending-products tracker: £7.13 price point, 2.33K orders and 19.34K units sold in its tracked 7-day window, ~£138K revenue. Caveat on recency: FindNiche states this snapshot was last updated September 1, 2026 (monthly cadence), so the "past 7 days" figure reflects late August/early Sept 2026, not the current week — noted plainly rather than presented as live-this-week data. Still indicates a sustained "build-your-own treat bundle" format doing well on TikTok Shop into early autumn 2026. Source: FindNiche, "Top Trending TikTok Pet Supplies products in United Kingdom" (data as of Sept 1, 2026).

**Bullets considered and rejected for lack of verifiable recency/sourcing:** Woof Gang Bakery's TikTok-influencer-dog treat collection (@tikatheiggy, @akirablackpit, @dexterthebalancingdog) — confirmed via Pet Age to have actually launched September 24, **2025**, over a year outside today's window, so excluded despite strong product/influencer specificity. An Etsy "Pet Portrait Treat Box" TikTok anecdote (1.8M views cited in a marketing blog) and an iHeartDogs "11 products everywhere in 2026" roundup both lacked a verifiable publish date after repeated searches, so were left out per the recency rule.
