**Window covered:** live-data searches run 2026-10-12; traditional press scoped to ~Sept 21 – Oct 12, 2026 (broadened to 21 days where needed).

## Research process / source attempts (so the gaps are explicit)

- **Tracked sources (Reddit r/dogs, r/DogAdvice):** Attempted `reddit.com/r/dogs/top.json?t=week` and `reddit.com/r/DogAdvice/top.json?t=week` via WebFetch. Both calls failed outright (DNS resolution error), as did every subsequent WebFetch attempt on any domain (Wikipedia, iheartdogs.com, petsmart.com, petsradar.com, sitmeanssit.com, aftership.com) — this looks like a WebFetch-tool/infrastructure outage this run rather than a Reddit-specific block, since even unrelated test domains failed. Per protocol, did not retry beyond once/twice per URL and fell back to WebSearch (whose results include synthesized page content, which partially compensates).
- **Reddit via secondary routes:** Tried `site:reddit.com/r/dogs` and `site:reddit.com/r/DogAdvice` searches and GummySearch (a Reddit-recommendation aggregator) as a proxy. These surfaced only evergreen gear recommendations (Kong toys, Ruffwear/2hounds/PetSafe harnesses) with no recent viral spike — steady-state brand loyalty, not a dated trend, so excluded below per the recency/trend-type rules.
- **Direct platform pages:** Ran `site:tiktok.com dog toy`, `site:instagram.com dog harness OR dog bandana`, and similar. No actual tiktok.com/instagram.com post or profile pages were indexed/returned by search — only third-party coverage and hashtag-analytics sites. Noted and moved on, as instructed.
- **Press coverage / general search:** This is where usable, dated signal was found (queries below).
- Total distinct queries run: 15+, well over the 3-query minimum, spanning all four source-type tiers.

## Findings

- **Joyride Dogs glow-in-the-dark Halloween harness & leash collection** — *Sustained trend* / *Press coverage*. A limited-run 2026 Halloween line (harnesses $59.95–$69.95, matching leashes $24.95) with full wraparound reflective/glow panels; the "Black Skulls" leash was already sold out on the brand's own site as of Sept 21, 2026. Coverage frames this alongside a broader "dog Halloween gear" wave for the season. (Haunted Attraction Network, "Dog Halloween Is Having a Moment in 2026, From Glow Harnesses to Viral Ghost Dogs," fall 2026 — exact byline date not retrievable due to fetch failure, but content references a Sept 21, 2026 stock event, placing publication in our window.)

- **Home Depot "Ghost Dog" LED Halloween decoration (Home Accents Holiday, ~2.5 ft, ~$40)** — *Single viral moment, with a recurring/meme-like pattern* / *Press coverage*. Shoppers are posing their real dogs next to the glowing ghost-dog yard decoration and daring viewers to spot the live one, circulating under tags like "Home Depot Halloween dogs" on TikTok; the line has expanded for 2026 to include a Skeleton-Costume variant ($79.98) and a "Puppy Greeter" variant ($44.98). (Same Haunted Attraction Network article, fall 2026.)

- **"Ghost Photoshoot" / #ghostdog bedsheet costume trend** — *Sustained trend* (recurring annually since 2020, resurging again this Halloween season) / *Press coverage + general search*. Dogs dressed in white sheets as "ghosts," set to Jack Stauber's "Oh Klahoma," with PetsRadar covering it as "the only pet trend you need to know about this Halloween" and a secondary (lower-confidence, AI-aggregator) source citing 33.5M views / 6.2M likes for the #ghostdog tag. Treat the specific view-count figure as unverified since it comes from a market-aggregator rather than TikTok itself, but the costume-trend pattern itself is corroborated across multiple outlets (PetsRadar, AOL/Brussels Griffon piece, PetSmart's 2026 costume guide noting similar low-restriction costume preferences).

- **Not qualifying / explicitly ruled out:** Snuffle mats and reflective dog gear show steady sales growth (Amazon/TikTok Shop trackers) but no dated viral spike in the window — excluded as evergreen rather than trend. Evergreen Reddit gear picks (Kong, Ruffwear) excluded for the same reason.

**Bottom line for the Etsy seller:** The actionable signal this week is overwhelmingly Halloween-costume/accessory-driven — glow-in-the-dark and reflective harness/leash sets, and "ghost" or spooky-novelty costume pieces that photograph well for a TikTok-style reveal shot — rather than any single breakout toy or treat product.
