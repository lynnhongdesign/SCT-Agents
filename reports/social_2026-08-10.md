## Agent 2: Social Media — Product Trend Findings (week of 2026-08-10)

**Method note up front:** Reddit's JSON endpoints (r/dogs, r/DogAdvice, r/DIY) were all blocked outright by this environment's network egress proxy on the first attempt each — not a 403 from Reddit itself, but a proxy-level `EGRESS_BLOCKED` — so no Reddit data could be retrieved (source type 1 exhausted per instructions, one retry each, moved on). Attempting to directly WebFetch individual TikTok and Instagram post/profile URLs (source type 2) hit the same `EGRESS_BLOCKED` wall for every domain tried (tiktok.com, instagram.com), and this turned out to be a blanket restriction — even neutral test domains (wikipedia.org, yahoo.com, sheknows.com) were blocked the same way, so no direct page-content fetch was possible for any source in this run. All findings below therefore come from WebSearch queries (source types 2–4), which return indexed titles/snippets/hashtag-view-counts from TikTok discover pages, TikTok Shop listings, and press coverage without needing a direct fetch. Where possible, recency was cross-checked by searching for explicit publish dates.

- **Automatic dog ball launcher** (USB-charged, infrared-sensor fetch machines, ~30 ft throw, multiple sellers) — **sustained-traction trend**. Recurring across many creators/accounts (@lopwert, @andnacreviews, @officialwillygooddog, @ben_approved, others) under #automaticballlauncher and #tiktokmademebuyit, with posts spanning into 2026. Source type: Direct-platform indexed content (TikTok video/discover listings surfaced via search) + general search. Recency: content actively surfacing under current 2026 hashtags at time of search (exact per-post dates not independently verifiable due to fetch block).

- **Cooling gel mats/pads for dogs** (non-electric, pressure-activated gel mats; retailer-specific versions from Aldi, Target, Petco) — **sustained-traction trend**, clearly seasonal. Multiple brands and creators posting under #dogcoolingmat, #petcoolingmat, #tiktokmademebuyit; search summaries specifically referenced posts from June–July 2026 continuing into the current summer season. Source type: Direct-platform indexed content (TikTok posts/discover pages) + general search.

- **FurLife flea/tick/mosquito pendant** (essential-oil-based clip-on pendant, attaches to any collar, marketed as 12-month protection, sold via TikTok Shop) — **sustained-traction trend** for a single specific branded product. Recurring reviews/how-to-attach content under #furlife, listed directly on TikTok Shop's product page. Source type: Direct-platform indexed content (TikTok Shop PDP + discover/tag pages) + general search.

- **Lick mats** (silicone enrichment mats for frozen/spread treats) — **sustained-traction trend**, long-running rather than newly emergent: the #lickmat hashtag alone shows ~355.8M views, with recurring recipe/ASMR-style content under #lickmatrecipe, #dogenrichment, #enrichmentfordogs. Source type: Direct-platform indexed content (TikTok hashtag/discover pages) + general search.

**Note on single viral moments:** A specific search was run for a discrete, newly-viral single-moment product story (as distinct from a recurring trend), and one strong candidate turned up — a silicone "Should We Go?" slow-feeder dog bowl that got press pickup (SheKnows, Yahoo Lifestyle, Daily Paws) after a creator's (@saaammage) TikTok went viral. However, the Daily Paws coverage was verified via search to be published February 17, 2023 — well outside even the broadened 21-day window — so it was excluded rather than presented as current. No other qualifying single-moment product story turned up within 21 days after broadening the search; everything current traced back to ongoing/recurring trends rather than one fresh viral spike.
