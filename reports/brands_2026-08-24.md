- **[Brand move] Citizens Pet launches "Unconditional," a premium air-dried dog food brand** (Aug 11, 2026 — PR Newswire/Morningstar). Citizens Pet debuted Unconditional at SuperZoo 2026, built around a proprietary 100%-air-dried process plus air-dried-blend and kibble-topper lines, positioned as fresh-food-quality nutrition without refrigeration or frozen shipping (18–24 month shelf life). It launched immediately into national distribution at PetSmart, Petco, and Pet Supplies Plus — notable for going straight to big-box shelf placement at launch rather than DTC-first.

- **[Brand move] The Farmer's Dog acquires Woof, expanding beyond food into enrichment** (Aug 6, 2026 — PR Newswire/Morningstar/Pet Age; just ahead of the primary window but the most recent verified dog-space M&A found after broad searching, so included). Woof (founded 2019) makes the Pupsicle enrichment toy, Refill/Wellness Pops, and the HonestChew dental-chew line; it will run as an independent division under The Farmer's Dog. Deal terms weren't disclosed, but the move signals the fresh-food subscription leader diversifying its portfolio into toys/wellness rather than just nutrition — worth watching for a solo Etsy seller in adjacent enrichment/chew categories.

- **[Brand move] SuperZoo 2026 (Aug 12–14, Las Vegas) produced a wave of dog product debuts** (Modern Dog Magazine SuperZoo roundup, published during the Aug 12–14 show window). Highlights: BetterBone unveiled a full brand refresh plus its new "BetterBone GO" chew line in the New Product Showcase; Brightkins launched the Kanoodle Pyramid Puzzle (4 spinning tiers, 22 hidden treat compartments) at booth #4331, alongside a first look at "Brightkins Talking Buttons" slated for a January 2027 launch; Atomic Bubbles introduced non-toxic Puppy Love bubbles made in St. Louis; Vetericyn launched a Relief Kits line for itchy/irritated skin, eyes, and ears. Useful competitive-set signal for handmade-adjacent enrichment/chew sellers on what's trending at the industry's biggest trade show.

- **[Brand move] Petwealth and Kennel Connection accelerate their diagnostic partnership** (Aug 20, 2026 — BusinessWire, corroborated via Yahoo Finance and Pet Age). Building on response at SuperZoo 2026, at-home PCR diagnostics/AI health-intelligence platform Petwealth is integrating clinical-grade pet health screening directly into Kennel Connection's software, which powers 5,500+ U.S. pet-care facilities (daycares, boarding, groomers, hotels). Every Petwealth report also connects owners to a licensed vet 24/7 via a telehealth tie-in with Pawp — an example of pet-health data/software converging with brick-and-mortar pet-care channels rather than pure product retail.
