## 1. Apparel & Wear

- **Market size:** Global dog apparel market estimated at **$1.22–1.39 billion in 2026**, growing at roughly a **10.1% CAGR** toward ~$3.0B by 2035; North America's broader pet clothing market is pegged at **$2.03B (2025) → $2.12B (2026)** by a separate estimate. Over 67% of pet owners report spending on functional/fashion dog clothing (Fortune Business Insights / Grand View Research / GlobalGrowthInsights market reports, 2026).
- **M&A:** Outdoor/sports gear company **Thule acquired Swiss dog-gear brand curli** for CHF 10.1M (~$12.9M), with an earnout up to CHF 6.8M (~$8.7M) tied to 2026 results; deal expected to close Q2 2026 (trade press/Tracxn snippet, 2026 — exact announcement date unconfirmed).
- **Brands to watch:** **Hooray House Pets** launched its "Country Club" Summer 2026 collection (apparel, accessories, toys) exclusively at Target starting July 5, 2026 — notable for a first-year brand landing a major mass-retail exclusive (Pet Age, Jul 5 2026). **Little Beast** — bold-print dog onesies/hoodies that grew from an 800-sq-ft NYC apartment startup into a recognized DTC apparel name (trade/consumer press, 2026).

## 2. Supplements & Wellness

- **Market size:** Global dog supplements market estimated at **$1,629.8 million in 2026**, growing at a **5.9% CAGR** to $2,731.76M by 2035; dogs hold ~63% share of the pet supplements category. Broader pet dietary supplements market pegged at **$5.11B in 2026**, CAGR 7.42% to $7.31B by 2031 (360 Research Reports / Precedence Research / Fortune Business Insights, 2026 reports).
- **Funding/M&A:** UK startup **Omni Pet raised £11M (~$14.5M)**, led by IW Capital and Redrice Ventures, to launch a natural "Ozempic-style" weight-loss supplement for dogs (Yahoo Finance/Green Queen, Jun 23 2026). **AlphaPet Ventures acquired Tierliebhaber**, Germany's leading pet-supplement/functional-snack brand — AlphaPet's 6th acquisition since 2020 (PetfoodIndustry/EQS News, Apr 1 2026). Separately, **Morgan Stanley Capital Partners acquired FoodScience** (VetriScience, Pet Naturals, DaVinci brands) from Wind Point Partners (PetfoodIndustry, 2026 — exact date unconfirmed).
- **Brands to watch:** **Omni Pet** — Dragons' Den-backed, fresh $14.5M raise, targeting obesity/allergies with a drug-free "Ozempic for dogs" positioning. **Shevega** — UK vegan, vet-formulated dog supplement brand that opened a public crowdfunding round for personalized canine nutrition (Vegconomist, Jun 1 2026).

## 3. Toys & Enrichment

- **Market size:** Global dog toys market estimated at **$4.42 billion in 2026**, rising to $7.67B by 2034 (7.12% CAGR); dogs represent ~67.6% of the broader pet toys market. The US **interactive/enrichment dog toys** sub-segment is roughly **$0.6 billion**, growing at 6.5% CAGR (BusinessResearchInsights / Grand View Research, 2026 reports).
- **M&A:** **Blue-9 acquired by Paw Prosper** (Feb 27, 2026), part of a broader wave of mid-sized toy and accessory brand acquisitions across Europe/North America in Q1–Q2 2026 (Tracxn, 2026).
- **Brands to watch:** **Woof** — its Pupsicle toy won a 2024 Pet Innovation Award and TIME Best Inventions honorable mention; the DTC brand has since expanded into 5,000+ retail doors including a nationwide PetSmart rollout (Businesswire, trend confirmed into 2026). **Brightkins** — brain-boosting enrichment/puzzle toy maker showing new SKUs (Kanoodle Pyramid Puzzle, lick mats) at Global Pet Expo 2026 (Modern Dog Magazine, 2026).

## 4. Food & Treats

- **Market size:** US pet treats market valued at **$11.25 billion in 2026**, projected to reach $15.84B by 2031 (7.1% CAGR); global dog food market estimated at **$80.34 billion in 2026**, growing to $117.22B by 2034 (4.84% CAGR) (Mordor Intelligence / Fortune Business Insights, 2026 reports).
- **M&A (active category — several deals in 2026):** **Pure Treats (maker of PureBites) acquired Primal Pet Foods** from Kinderhook Industries (Pet Age/Yahoo Finance, Feb 17 2026). **Agrolimen acquired fresh dog food brand Ollie** at an enterprise value exceeding $600M (PetfoodIndustry, Feb 2026). **RCL Foods acquired Martin & Martin for $41 million**, expanding into wet food, biscuits and treats (PetfoodIndustry, 2026). **Assisi Pet Care acquired Sniffers Pet Care**, owner of natural dog treat brand NAW (PetfoodIndustry, 2026). Overall, pet food/treat M&A "remained resilient" through H1 2026 (Pet Food Processing, 2026).
- **Brands to watch:** **SCRUMPF** — launching a new air-dried dog food range (five SKUs including Air-Dried Goat/Venison) at Interzoo 2026 (PetfoodIndustry, 2026). **Tucker's** — acquired Nuggets Healthy Eats to expand into bone broth, with formal launch planned at SUPERZOO 2026 (PetfoodIndustry, 2026).

## 5. Accessories (collars/leashes/carriers/beds)

- **Market size:** Global dog collars/leashes/harnesses market estimated at **$6.8 billion in 2026**, growing to $12.1B by 2033 (8.6% CAGR); broader global pet accessories market pegged at **$7.6 billion in 2026** by Grand View Research, rising to $12.3B by 2033 (note: estimates vary widely by source/scope — one outlier source cited implausible figures that appear to be a data error and were excluded) (Persistence Market Research / Grand View Research, 2026 reports).
- **M&A:** As noted above, **Thule's acquisition of curli** (harness/leash-focused Swiss brand, CHF 10.1M/~$12.9M, Q2 2026 close) is arguably as much an accessories deal as an apparel one. As of Jan 2026, **89 pet-accessories-sector companies had received funding, with the US drawing $156M** in cumulative investment (Tracxn, Jan 2026).
- **Brands to watch:** **VETRESKA** — unveiled its "Circuit Collection," motorsport-inspired pet accessories co-designed with F1 driver Ollie Bearman, available July 16, 2026 via vetreskanyc.com and global retailers — notable for celebrity/sport crossover positioning (PR Newswire, Jul 16 2026). **Mars Petcare** expanded its premium accessories line with AI-enabled smart feeding systems and interactive toys, signaling legacy players pushing into the smart-accessories crossover space (trade press, Feb 2026).

## 6. Tech & Gadgets (GPS trackers, smart feeders, etc.)

- **Market size:** Estimates vary sharply by scope: broad "pet tech" market pegged between **$10.63B and $24.96B in 2026** depending on source (CAGR range 14.2%–24.9%). Narrower segments: **GPS tracker/smart dog collar market ~$0.72 billion in 2026**, growing to $1.64B by 2035 (9.4% CAGR); **automatic/smart pet feeder market ~$3.26 billion in 2026**, with the fastest sub-segment CAGR at 12.8% (Grand View Research / market.us / BusinessResearchInsights, 2026 reports). Pet wearables specifically are cited as a **$3.8 billion industry in 2026**, projected to hit $11.4B by 2033.
- **Funding/M&A:** **Fi is bringing SpaceX Starlink direct-to-cell satellite connectivity to its smart dog collar** — a first for consumer pet tech, per an exclusive report; Fi is backed by Lerer Hippeau Ventures (Fortune, Jul 8 2026 — exclusive). **Tractive** reported **€100M ARR**, cited as the clearest proof point of a recurring-revenue business model in pet tech (New Market Pitch, 2026). **MOVA Pets raised a Series A** to build AI-driven "digital twin" pet health platforms (New Market Pitch, 2026). Total pet-tech VC/PE investment exceeded **$850M in 2024** and has continued rising into 2026, with investors favoring hardware-plus-subscription models over pure device plays.
- **Brands to watch:** **Fi** — the Starlink satellite integration is a genuine technical leap (rural/off-grid GPS coverage) and just broke as exclusive news. **Halo Collar** (co-founded by César Millán) — scaled to **$100M in revenue** on wireless containment technology, an established but still fast-growing name (U.S. Chamber of Commerce/CO—, 2026).
