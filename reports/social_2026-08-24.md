# Agent 2 — Social Media Product Trends
**Date compiled:** August 24, 2026

**Source access note (read first):** Reddit (both tracked hubs `r/dogs` and `r/DogAdvice`, their `.json` endpoints, and the fallback subs `r/DIY`, `r/Etsy`, `r/dogtraining`) returned an immediate "unable to fetch" error on every attempt — this is a full domain block in this environment, not a rate limit, so no Reddit-sourced data could be gathered despite being the priority source. Direct WebFetch of TikTok, Instagram, Newsweek, and several blog/press URLs was also blocked by the network egress proxy (`EGRESS_BLOCKED`), not by the sites themselves. As a result, everything below is built from WebSearch results and snippets (source types 2–4), not full-page fetches — flagged per bullet. Ran 7 distinct search queries across varied phrasing (TikTok/Instagram site-restricted, product-specific, press-coverage, and general "viral dog product" queries) to meet the minimum research bar.

---

- **Pet steam/deshedding brush (e.g., RadPaws 3-in-1, FEELNEEDY, CozyPaws, Furgo "steam brush," Owleys "Vapor")** — *Sustained trend* — *General search / Press coverage (snippet only — full pages blocked by egress proxy)*. An electric brush with a built-in mist/steam function for deshedding, static reduction, and coat conditioning. Independent reviews/blog posts on this product category appeared repeatedly across 2026 (RadPaws review ~Feb 28, Enchanting Pets ~Apr 7, Pet and Life ~Jun 18, CozyPaws UK ~Jul 10), each covering a different competing brand — a pattern of sustained, multi-brand traction rather than one post. Corroborated by multiple live TikTok "discover" tag pages (dog-steam-brush, dog-steam-brush-review, pet-steam-brush-review) turning up independently in search, and by a pet-dropshipping trend tracker (Sell The Trend) listing "Steam Pet Brush" among currently-hot pet products as of early August 2026.

- **Dog cooling mat (self-cooling/ice-silk gel mat, no water/electricity/freezer needed)** — *Sustained trend* — *Direct platform page (TikTok Shop / TikTok video snippets)*. Multiple individual TikTok Shop videos surfaced directly in search (e.g., `@hqsyzbgqu4b`, `@dogfriendlyco`, `@three_reasons_why`), each independently using hashtags `#coolingmat`, `#tiktokmademebuyit`, `#dogaccessories`. Full video pages could not be fetched (blocked shell/proxy), so detail is snippet-only, but the pattern spans several creators and retailer-specific variants (Lesure, Barkly, Aldi, Walmart, Petco versions) rather than a single post — consistent with a recurring seasonal (summer 2026) trend, not an isolated moment.

- **4-in-1 automatic paw cleaner / pet dental powder / dog bath massage brush** — *Single viral moment (weak/unverified)* — *General search (snippet only — one aggregator source, "Sell The Trend" dropship tracker, could not be independently fetched or corroborated elsewhere)*. These appeared together in one weekly-updated dropshipping trend list alongside a claimed "GhostGuard™ Dog Mask" product that could not be verified anywhere else (a direct search for "GhostGuard dog mask" turned up no matching product on Amazon, Etsy, or elsewhere) — flagging this bullet as lower-confidence and likely reflecting a dropship-marketing list rather than genuine organic virality. Included for completeness but should be weighted less than the two bullets above.

**What came up empty:** No qualifying single-viral-moment dog product post could be confirmed from Reddit (blocked entirely) or from direct Instagram/TikTok profile fetches (all attempted platform-page fetches were proxy-blocked before rendering content — e.g., `tiktok.com/discover/viral-pet-products-2026` returned only an egress error, not an empty shell with metadata). Robot/AI companion dog toys (WowWee Dog-E, KEYi Loona) surfaced via a Newsweek "Best Pet Toy 2026" reader's-choice piece but that page also could not be fetched for confirmation, and reads as an annual awards list rather than a fresh viral moment, so it was left out of the main bullets.
