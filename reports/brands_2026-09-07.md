- **[Brand move] The Farmer's Dog launches "Crunchy Biscuits" treat line** — On September 1, 2026, human-grade dog food leader The Farmer's Dog introduced its first standalone treat line: all-natural biscuits made by the company's on-staff board-certified veterinary nutritionists, in three flavors (Beef & Sweet Potato, Goat Milk & Blueberries, Peanut Butter & Pumpkin), 9 calories per biscuit, with a snap line for portioning. No added sugar, fillers, or artificial preservatives. Notable as the brand's first major push from mealtime into treat occasions, sold via its own site and Walmart.com. *(Source: PR Newswire / Pet Food Processing, Sept. 1, 2026)*

- **[Brand move] Canidae partners with country artist Walker Hayes to launch "Heartland" line tied to Red Cross donation** — Announced August 26, 2026 (National Dog Day), Canidae debuted a new dog food line, Canidae Heartland, alongside an original song by Hayes ("Love Is a Four-Legged Word — Heartland Version"). 1% of retail purchases of the Heartland line are donated to the American Red Cross to support communities facing emergencies — a cause-marketing partnership combining celebrity collaboration with a charitable tie-in. *(Source: PetfoodIndustry.com / PR Newswire, Aug. 26, 2026)*

- **[Brand move] Petco reports Q2 FY2026 results — soft dog category offsets cat strength** — Reported September 2, 2026: sales of $1.5 billion with comparable sales up 0.6% (second consecutive positive quarter); adjusted EBITDA of $122 million ($115 million excluding a $6.8 million tariff-refund benefit). Management specifically flagged that the **dog category remained soft**, reflecting lower adoptions industry-wide, while cat outperformed on new products and ownership-mix shifts. Q3 guidance calls for 0.4%–1% YoY growth; full-year outlook reaffirmed at flat-to-1.5% sales growth and $415–430 million adjusted EBITDA. Relevant signal for dog-focused sellers: category-wide demand softness even as the retailer overall stabilizes. *(Source: Petco Investor Relations / Investing.com earnings call transcript, Sept. 2, 2026)*

- **[Brand move] Lil Luv Dog expands into UK retail with Selfridges London exclusive** — On August 25, 2026, LA-based "clean" pet care brand Lil Luv Dog (backed by a $1M pre-seed round from Ani VC, Halogen Ventures, Twenty Two Ventures, and angels including PrettyLitter founder Daniel Rotman and former Zesty Paws CEO Steve Ball) held its UK launch event and debuted as a Selfridges exclusive — its first international retail placement, marking expansion beyond DTC/US into a new geographic and channel. *(Source: Retail Gazette / FashionNetwork USA, Aug. 25, 2026)*
