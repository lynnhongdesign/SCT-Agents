## Top signals this week
- **The Farmer's Dog is consolidating the wellness/enrichment space and going mainstream** — it completed its first-ever acquisition (Woof, refillable enrichment toys/chews) on Sept 18, then landed a multi-year deal to become the NFL's first official pet food partner on Sept 22. Two moves in one week from the category's dominant DTC player signal where premium-brand capital is flowing: nutrition → enrichment/wellness bundling, and DTC → mainstream-audience marketing.
- **Celebrity/DTC dog food brand Kismet broke into brick-and-mortar** (Chrissy Teigen/John Legend brand, ~1,000 PetSmart stores, ~Sept 26) — a concrete data point that online-only dog food brands are actively pursuing specialty retail as a growth channel, not just staying DTC.
- **Fresh/subscription dog food still pulling in seed capital** — DASH Dog Food (the "ButcherBox for Pets" rebrand) closed an oversubscribed $1.5M seed (Sept 22) specifically to scale subscription acquisition and fresh-frozen R&D, echoing the segment's $4.1B (2026) valuation and 10%+ CAGR outlook.
- **Interactive/motion toys and functional wearables are the sustained TikTok trend, not a single viral spike** — QGI's unpredictable-motion dog ball and Spark Paws' evaporative cooling vest both show multi-creator, recurring-hashtag traction into late September, more actionable for a small-batch maker than any one-off viral moment (none qualified this week).
- **Pet "longevity" is emerging as its own supplement subcategory** — Fera Pets launched an NMN/MitoPrime daily chew targeting cellular aging (Sept 15), alongside earlier-2026 mega-rounds for longevity-drug company Loyal ($100M Series C) — a category worth watching if considering wellness-adjacent product lines.
