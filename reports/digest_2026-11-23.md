### 2. Supplements & Wellness
- US pet supplements market sized at $2.9B, 3% CAGR forecast 2026–2030, per Packaged Facts/Freedonia Group's 11th edition report (snippet only). Growth described as "moderating" per a separate Pet Food Industry piece.
- Funding: **Omni Pet** (UK, dog weight-loss supplements) raised £11M (~$14.5M), reported June 23, 2026, after ~10x YoY revenue growth. **Oh Norman!** (Kaley Cuoco co-founded) closed a $2.08M round, oversubscribed 39% against a $1.5M target, with Mars Petcare among investors.
- Watch: Omni Pet (fastest growth cited) and Oh Norman! (celebrity-backed, strategic Mars interest).

### 3. Toys & Enrichment
- Total US/global pet toys market ~$11.6B in 2026, growing to $22.4B by 2033 (9.9% CAGR, Grand View Research, snippet only). Enrichment-specific sub-segment estimates range wildly ($126M–$1.18B) depending on publisher definition — treat cautiously.
- No pure toy-category funding/M&A found. Adjacent: **Tombot** (AI robotic companion dog) closed a ~$7M Series A3 growth round, targeting Fall 2026 consumer launch (TheNextWeb, June 25, 2026).
- Watch: **SodaPup** (mainstreaming food-enrichment puzzles) and **Tombot** (strong VC interest in robotic companion category).

### 4. Food & Treats
- US dog treat sales ~$7.3B in 2026 (+2.4% YoY); freeze-dried dog food up 17.3% to $416.6M (Pet Food Processing's "State of the Pet Food/Treat Industry 2026," snippet only). Total US pet spend hit $158B in 2025 per APPA (dated March 26, 2026).
- M&A: Agrolimen/Affinity Petcare's acquisition of fresh dog-food brand Ollie (~$600M+ valuation, reported Feb 2026) remains the most recent notable deal — 9+ months old, no fresher dog-food M&A surfaced this week.
- Watch: **Ollie** (premium fresh format, just acquired at a strong valuation) and **Happy Howl** (DTC gently-cooked food, Mars Petcare investment for "next phase of growth").

### 5. Accessories (collars/leashes/carriers/beds)
- Collar/leash/harness market ~$6.8B in 2026 (manufacturer-linked source, treat as directional). Pet carriers market more reliably sized at $936.95M in 2026 (Fortune Business Insights). Pet beds at $5.84B (2025) → $8.85B by 2032 (Persistence Market Research).

### 6. Tech & Gadgets (GPS trackers, smart feeders, etc.)
