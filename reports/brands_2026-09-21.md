- **[Brand move] The Farmer's Dog completes acquisition of Woof (M&A)** — Completed September 18, 2026 (deal originally announced August 6, 2026), per PR Newswire/multiple outlets (snippet only — full pages blocked, PRNewswire/PetfoodIndustry/Pet Age). Woof will continue operating independently under its own brand and team but gain access to The Farmer's Dog's resources. Stated rationale: expand The Farmer's Dog beyond its core fresh-food subscription business into a broader pet-wellness platform spanning nutrition, enrichment and play.

- **[Brand move] The Farmer's Dog named NFL's first-ever Official Pet Food Partner (Partnership)** — Announced September 9, 2026, per PR Newswire/Athlon/Yahoo Sports (snippet only — full pages blocked). Multi-year deal makes The Farmer's Dog the NFL's first pet-food partner; campaign stars New York Giants RB Cam Skattebo and his senior rescue dog Duke (plus his dachshund puppies Cheech and Daizy), both described as longtime Farmer's Dog customers. Activations planned around Super Bowl LXI, the NFL Combine, and NFL Draft, plus tie-ins to My Cause My Cleats, Salute to Service and NFL FLAG programs.

- **[Brand move] Blue Buffalo launches limited-edition football-season dog treats (Product launch)** — Published September 8, 2026, per BusinessWire/PetfoodIndustry/Pet Food Processing (snippet only — full pages blocked). General Mills' Blue Buffalo brand debuted two new limited-edition SKUs: "Game Day Nudges" (Steak Grillers, Chicken Jerky Cut, Chicken Pot Pie flavors, real meat, no artificial preservatives) available now nationwide, and football-shaped "Health Bars Touchdown Treats" (Bacon, Egg & Cheese flavor, oven-baked with real USA bacon) arriving nationwide November 2. Framed as letting dogs "join" tailgates/watch parties — a seasonal marketing tie-in play.

- **[Brand move] Petco reports second consecutive quarter of positive comps; dog category still soft (Sales/performance)** — Results reported September 2, 2026; earnings call September 9, 2026, per Motley Fool/Investing.com/Petco corporate site (snippet only — full pages blocked). Petco (NASDAQ: WOOF) posted a 0.6% company-wide comparable-sales increase — its second straight positive-comp quarter — with debt reportedly down ~15%. However, the core dog category "remained soft," which management tied to industry-wide declines in dog adoption rates rather than demand for existing dog owners. A bright spot: veterinary-diet sales showed double-digit growth in both the dog and cat categories, aided by in-store vet recommendations. Management reportedly expects adoption trends — and by extension dog-category softness — to begin recovering in 2027.
