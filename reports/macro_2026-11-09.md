**Week of 2026-11-09**

Every bullet below is therefore sourced from WebSearch result snippets only, flagged inline as "(snippet only)". Dollar figures should be treated as directional — market-research firms disagree substantially on scope/methodology for every category. Despite 25+ distinct queries across all 6 categories, very little hard news was found dated specifically within the Oct 26–Nov 9, 2026 window; most substantive category data clusters from January–August 2026, which is flagged per category below.

### 1. Apparel & Wear
- Global dog apparel market estimated at ~$1.2B in 2026, projected to reach ~$1.95B by 2033 (7.13% CAGR) per DIResearch; broader "pet clothing" market pegged at $6.3–6.6B in 2026 by other firms (5.3% CAGR) — figures diverge sharply by scope (snippet only — market-research aggregator reports, 2026).
- Kith (streetwear label) launched its first pet collection in partnership with NY dog brand Wagwear — reported April 2026 (FashionNetwork, snippet only).
- No funding/M&A specific to apparel found dated within the Oct 26–Nov 9 window.
- **Brands to watch:** pupp st. (NYC dog streetwear label; restocked its "Hester Shirt" and expanded into windbreakers, May 2026; landed on Chewy Sept 2025 after building an organic social following — Pet Age, snippet only) and WOOF (positions as everyday streetwear vs. novelty dog-themed graphics, cited alongside Kith's category entry).

### 2. Supplements & Wellness
- Packaged Facts' "Pet Supplements in the U.S., 11th Edition" reports category growth slowed to under 5% in 2024 and further in 2025, with 2–3% growth projected going forward, citing economic pressure and a flat dog population (Pet Food Industry trade write-up, 2026, exact publish date unconfirmed — snippet only).
- Per Packaged Facts' December 2025 pet-owner survey, 65% of pet owners said they cut spending on "extras" (including supplements) in the past year — a headwind specific to this category.
- Counter-data point: Cascadia Pet Industry Insights values the U.S. dog supplement segment at over $3B and projects 10.7% CAGR through 2028 — notably more bullish than Packaged Facts (snippet only).
- **Brand to watch:** Oh Norman! (Kaley Cuoco's pet wellness brand), reported to have raised ~$2.08M in its latest round — notable for celebrity-driven positioning in a segment otherwise decelerating (date unconfirmed, snippet only).

### 3. Toys & Enrichment
- Narrow "interactive enrichment" dog toy segment valued near $126M in 2025, projected to reach $292M by 2034 (13.0% CAGR); broader "pet toys" market estimates for 2026 range from ~$4.7B to ~$11.8B depending on publisher scope (snippet only). Dogs reportedly account for ~67.8% of global pet-toy market share in 2026 per one report.
- Counter-signal: GRIPS Intelligence (tracking online retail sales) shows interactive/durable dog toy sales down 20–50% y/y in 2025, with a further 10–20% decline forecast for 2026 — suggesting retail-level softness even as broader forecasts show growth.
- M&A: The Farmer's Dog acquired Woof Pet (maker of the Pupsicle slow-feeder toy and dental products) — reported August 2026, terms undisclosed (Preqin, snippet only). Separately, Waukesha-based SportPet Designs acquired California dog-toy maker Mammoth Pet Products — BizTimes coverage suggests an October 2026 date, unconfirmed (snippet only).
- **Brands to watch:** Woof Pet (now owned by The Farmer's Dog — validates slow-feeder/dental-toy crossover with food brands) and Tug-E-Nuff (UK tug-toy specialist reporting 16% export sales growth in 2025, opening US wholesale distribution to independent retailers in 2026).

### 4. Food & Treats
- Global pet treats market estimated at $38.4B in 2026, projected to reach $58.7B by 2031 (8.9% CAGR) per MarketsandMarkets; dog food alone estimated anywhere from $45.6B to $106.8B for 2026 depending on publisher (snippet only — figures don't reconcile across firms).
- M&A remained very active through 2026, though all dated earlier in the year (nothing within Oct 26–Nov 9 window): Nasta Pet Food acquired FirstMate (Feb 2026, backed by a €120M HIG WhiteHorse loan, combined group targeting ~€200M 2026 revenue); Pure Treats acquired Primal Pet Foods/PureBites (Feb 2026); Agrolimen acquired fresh/human-grade brand Ollie (Feb 2026); RCL Foods agreed to acquire Martin & Martin (Husky, Pamper, Beeno, Bob Martin) for $41M (April 2026, pending approval); "I and love and you" acquired Made by Nacho (Mar 17, 2026).
- Trade commentary (Pet Food Industry, 2026) notes many PE-backed pet-consumables companies from the early-2020s are nearing typical hold-period end, suggesting more consolidation ahead.
- **Brands to watch:** Ollie (fresh, human-grade dog food; Agrolimen acquisition signals continued strategic validation of the premium-fresh segment) and Benny's Bowl (raised $1.4M pre-Series A, Feb 2026, Atomic Capital-led).

### 5. Accessories (collars/leashes/carriers/beds)
- Pet accessories market estimates for 2026 vary widely by scope: Grand View Research ~$7.2–7.6B (to $12.3B by 2033); Deep Market Insights ~$7.15B (6.9% CAGR to $10.3B by 2031); broader "dog accessories" definition (MarkWide Research) as high as $18.7B for 2026 (snippet only). Collars & leashes represent the largest product-type share within the category per multiple reports.
- No dog-bed- or carrier-specific institutional funding/M&A surfaced for 2026 despite multiple search variations; closest leads were crowdfunding (e.g., Paw.com's AirX Foam cooling dog bed Kickstarter, ~105% funded, targeting April 2026 shipping) rather than VC/PE activity — this category appears thin on funding news this period.
- Funding: Fable, maker of a hands-free leash ("Magic Link"), reportedly raised $9M in a Series A led by 14W with Female Founders Fund and Slow Ventures participating — exact date unconfirmed (secondary aggregator, snippet only).
- **Brands to watch:** Fable (differentiated hands-free leash hardware, notable VC backer list) and Heart Pup (hybrid sling/carrier-tote design, currently angel-stage funding per CB Insights).

### 6. Tech & Gadgets (GPS trackers, smart feeders)
- 2026 "pet tech" market-size estimates diverge sharply by publisher: Persistence Market Research ~$15.9B; Global Market Insights ~$19.1B; Grand View Research internally inconsistent ($10.3B vs. a $23.54B-by-2033 headline) (snippet only). Grand View projects the smart-feeder sub-segment growing at 12.8% CAGR, 2026–2033. Pet GPS trackers specifically projected to grow from $2.62B (2025) to $8.5B by 2034 (13.97% CAGR, Value Market Research).
- Major category shakeup: Whistle (GPS tracker brand) was discontinued around August 2025, with Tractive reportedly acquiring the product line/migrating 500K+ subscribers — reshaping competitive dynamics heading into 2026 (snippet only, dates imprecise).
- Recent brand moves (none within Oct 26–Nov 9 window): Fi announced expansion to 38 countries across North America and Europe (June 3, 2026); Halo Collar launched "Precision+" positioning system (May 18, 2026). Funding: SATELLAI (satellite-positioning smart collar) raised an estimated $4.2M Series A (~January 2026); Traini raised $7.5M for an AI "pet emotional intelligence" smart collar (late 2025/early 2026, date imprecise).
- **Brands to watch:** Fi (premium LTE GPS collar, continued aggressive international expansion) and Traini/SATELLAI (AI-driven emotion- and health-sensing collars — a differentiated step beyond basic GPS tracking).
