# Weekly Dog Industry Intelligence Digest — August 17, 2026

## Top signals this week
- **Cooling vests/bandanas and freeze-dried treats are the two most directly actionable social trends right now** — both are sustained (not one-off) trends visible across multiple TikTok creators/brand accounts, and both are hand-makeable at small-batch scale, making them the closest fit to what an Etsy seller could realistically launch this month. *(Social Media)*
- **The Farmer's Dog acquired Woof** (the brand behind the viral "Pupsicle" enrichment product), its first move outside core nutrition — a signal that well-funded food/subscription brands see enrichment/toys as the next adjacency worth buying into, not just building organically. *(Brands & Companies)*
- **SuperZoo 2026 (Aug 12–14, Las Vegas)** produced a wave of 2,000+ new product debuts, with a clear cluster around material-safety innovation (BetterBone's nylon-free chews) and functional wellness (Bernie's Best liquid omegas, Citizens Pet's air-dried food) — worth noting as a "what buyers are being told matters" signal even for non-exhibiting small sellers. *(Macro Stats — Toys, Supplements, Food; Brands & Companies)*
- **Pet Valu's Q2 2026 results show premiumization outrunning foot traffic**: revenue +3.6% and net income +14.3% YoY, but same-store transactions down 1.4% — retailers are growing by getting existing customers to spend more per visit, not by attracting more shoppers. Relevant read-through for Etsy pricing/positioning: don't chase volume, chase perceived value. *(Brands & Companies)*
- **Pet tech remains the fastest-growing category by far** (global market ~$25B, 24.9% CAGR) though funding is concentrated in vet software rather than consumer hardware — a category to watch, not one an Etsy seller can easily enter directly.
