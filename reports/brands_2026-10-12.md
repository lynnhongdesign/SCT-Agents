## Findings

- **[Brand move]** **The Farmer's Dog completed its acquisition of Woof** — closed Sept 18–19, 2026 (petfoodindustry.com / feedandadditive.com / trysignalbase.com, snippet only — full pages blocked). Woof (founded 2019) makes refillable "wellness systems" combining nutrition with enrichment, led by its flagship Pupsicle product. Financial terms undisclosed. Woof continues operating as an independent division (not absorbed) — stated strategy is to keep Woof's existing line while co-developing new products. Marks Farmer's Dog's first move beyond fresh food into the enrichment/toy category.

- **[Brand move]** **BARK and Girl Scouts of the USA launched "Patch Pals"** — announced Sept 9, 2026 (Business Wire via Finviz, snippet only). First-ever Girl Scout cookie made for dogs (blueberry-muffin flavor) — a licensed co-brand product, not just a sponsorship. Launches nationally Jan 2027, with samples going into BarkBox/Super Chewer boxes that month. This is BARK's third major co-brand tie-up this year (after Dunkin' in August, Liquid Death on Sept 2), signaling a deliberate partnership-heavy growth push while core subscriber revenue is under pressure.

- **[Brand move]** **BARK Inc. reported fiscal Q1 2027 results** — reported Aug 6, 2026 (Business Wire / StreetInsider, snippet only). Revenue $78.8M, down 23.4% YoY (high end of guidance); DTC revenue fell 25.2% to $66.7M; BARK Air grew 37% to $3.2M. Despite the revenue decline, net income swung positive to $0.75M (vs. $7.0M loss prior year, aided by $7.4M in tariff refunds); adjusted EBITDA $0.6M. Ad/marketing spend nearly halved ($9.5M vs. $15.2M prior year) — a clear "shrink to profitability" pattern worth tracking given BARK's market visibility.
