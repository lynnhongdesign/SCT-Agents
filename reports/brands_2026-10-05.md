- **[Brand move] The Farmer's Dog acquires Woof, its first-ever acquisition, to expand beyond fresh food into enrichment/wellness.** Completed Sept 18, 2026. Woof (founded 2019, maker of refillable enrichment products like the "Pupsicle") was ranked #3 overall and #1 in consumer products on the 2025 Inc. 5000 fastest-growing private companies list. Woof will keep operating independently under its own brand/leadership while tapping The Farmer's Dog's resources. Financial terms not disclosed. (Source: Pulse2 / PetFoodIndustry.com / Latham & Watkins press note, Sept 18–19, 2026 — snippet only for PetFoodIndustry.com, full page blocked)

- **[Brand move] DASH Dog Food raises $1.5M seed round led by Voltage Ventures to expand fresh-frozen dog food.** Founded by Bobby Quirk (ButcherBox's first employee and former VP of Operations), Boston/Portland-based DASH makes premium fresh-frozen dog food using human-food-industry sourcing standards. Round was oversubscribed; Western Technology Investment and individual DTC/cold-chain/retail investors also participated. Voltage Ventures' Marissa Bertorelli and ButcherBox CEO Mike Salguero joined DASH's board. Funds will go toward the subscription business, customer acquisition, expanded product development, and new seafood and turkey recipes. (Source: Pulse2 / PetFoodIndustry.com, ~Sept 21–22, 2026 — snippet only for PetFoodIndustry.com, full page blocked)

- **[Brand move] Wuffes launches a Dental Powder line built on proprietary "SuperYolk IgY" technology, its first entry into oral care.** The egg-yolk-derived antibody targets gingipain, the bacterial enzyme behind most canine oral disease; it's a bone-broth-flavored powder sprinkled on food, no brushing required — positioned as first-of-its-kind in the U.S. canine dental-supplement market. Notably, Wuffes (founded 2020 as a DTC mobility-supplement startup) reports 3-year revenue growth exceeding 1,000% and recently expanded into PetSmart and Tractor Supply retail. (Source: PetFoodIndustry.com, Sept 22, 2026 — snippet only, full page blocked; corroborated by Briefglance.com coverage)

- **[Brand move] Nulo expands its autumn product range into Arcaplanet stores in Italy**, a channel/market expansion for the U.S. pet food brand into one of Italy's largest pet retail chains. Limited detail available beyond the headline confirmation. (Source: PetFoodIndustry.com, Sept 22, 2026 — snippet only, full page blocked; specific article could not be independently verified beyond headline)

- **[Brand move] Saint Rocco's Treats (handcrafted, 7-ingredient dog treats) partners with PAWS Chicago**, becoming the shelter's official training/enrichment treat sponsor. Saint Rocco's will donate 1,000 lbs of treats over 12 months to support positive-reinforcement training, enrichment, and medical care for dogs awaiting adoption at PAWS Chicago's Pippen Fasseas Adoption Center and Kocourek Medical Center, plus citywide adoption events. Relevant to small-batch sellers as a cause-marketing/visibility model a solo Etsy brand could emulate at small scale. (Source: PetFoodIndustry.com, Sept 21, 2026 — snippet only, full page blocked)
