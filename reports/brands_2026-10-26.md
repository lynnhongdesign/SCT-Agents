- **[Brand move] Warhound raises $200M seed at $1.2B valuation to genetically engineer/clone military working dogs** (reported Oct 22–23, 2026, via Morning Brew, TechFundingNews, WOWTALE, Benzinga — snippet only). Founded by Palmer Luckey (Anduril co-founder) and Ben Lamm (Colossal Biosciences co-founder), the Texas-based startup plans to clone and gene-edit dogs from top working breeds for sale to the Department of Defense, police forces, and first responders. Reported investors include Sequoia Capital, 8VC, Corner Ventures, and In-Q-Tel, though no lead investor was named. No dogs exist yet — first births are expected in early 2027, with service within two years. An outlier in scale (one of the largest dog-adjacent raises in recent memory), though it's a defense/biotech play rather than a consumer product — worth flagging for context on where big pet-sector capital is flowing.

- **[Brand move] Nulo expands into 179 Hollywood Feed stores across 19 states** (Petfood Industry, undated in search index but describes a soft launch beginning Oct. 19, 2026 — snippet only; petfoodindustry.com/news-newsletters/pet-food-news/news/15837818). The pet food brand's lineup — including its grain-free Freestyle line, oven-baked Culinary Harvest recipes, and a selection of wet foods — begins a soft launch Oct. 19 in select Georgia, Indiana, and Ohio markets, with full rollout to all 179 Hollywood Feed locations starting Nov. 2, 2026. A concrete near-term retail-expansion case study for how larger players scale into specialty pet retail chains.

- **[Brand move] DASH Dog Food closes $1.5M seed round led by Voltage Ventures** (Pulse2 / Petfood Industry / Dealroom, dated Sept 22, 2026 — snippet only; roughly a month outside the ideal 7–14 day window but included for its specificity, since fresher funding news was otherwise thin). The fresh-frozen dog food brand, founded by former ButcherBox VP of Operations Bobby Quirk, was oversubscribed with participation from Western Technology Investment and individual investors. Funds go toward subscriber growth, customer acquisition, and new seafood and turkey recipes (~10% of the round earmarked for R&D on those formulations). DASH spun out from ButcherBox in June 2025 and reports 130% revenue growth since. Mike Salguero (ButcherBox CEO) and Marissa Bertorelli (Voltage Ventures) are joining the board.

- **[Brand move] The Farmer's Dog completes acquisition of pet wellness/enrichment brand Woof** (announced August 2026, deal reported as completed Sept 24, 2026 per Petfood Industry/GlobalPETS — snippet only; also outside the ideal window but the clearest M&A item found). Woof will operate as an independent division under The Farmer's Dog, marking the fresh-dog-food unicorn's expansion beyond food into the enrichment/wellness category. Financial terms were not disclosed.
