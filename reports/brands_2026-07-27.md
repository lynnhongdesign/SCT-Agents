Built by the team behind Super Bowl-scale AI ad campaigns, now retrained on pet-specific data. Three "First Movers" pilot programs are open now (campaign scaling, conversion commerce assets, always-on social content); public debut is set for SuperZoo 2026 (Aug 12–14, Las Vegas). Relevant for small sellers as a preview of how AI content-generation tools are being commercialized specifically for pet product marketing. *(snippet only — full page blocked; EIN Presswire / Petfood Industry)*

- **[Brand move] Just for Pets & DOG expand British-made product partnership (channel expansion)** — UK pet retailer Just for Pets and its accessories sister brand DOG (known for Cotswolds-made leather collars/leads and Yorkshire-made tweed ranges) announced an expanded partnership around **July 23–24, 2026** to grow their British-made assortment, and are actively recruiting additional small UK manufacturers across leather goods, textiles, ceramics, grooming, and treats to bring into the retail range. This is a concrete channel-expansion signal for artisan/handmade makers — a large retailer publicly seeking small-batch domestic manufacturers as suppliers. *(snippet only — full page blocked; Pet Gazette / Pet Business World)*

Separately, Omni reported roughly 200% sales growth over the prior year.
